The grey wave is "how much the board is shaking overall" (all three axes combined). The red line is the threshold for counting a step — every time the wave spikes above the red line, that's one step.

1.30
Sitting still, the total shake is about 1 (that's gravity), so the threshold has to be a bit above 1. Set it too low and a tiny hand wobble counts as a step; set it too high and real steps get missed — shake the board and slide the threshold around to find what works best.
