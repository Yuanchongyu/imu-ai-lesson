STEP 1 Connect the board

Open this page in Chrome or Edge (Web Serial needs one of those). The board needs imu_stream.ino flashed onto it.

LIVE Accelerometer

Red = X, green = Y, blue = Z. Shake the board and the lines should jump.

STEP 2 Record your moves

Name a move, hold the board, do the move, and hit Record — each take is 1.5 seconds. Record at least 15 takes per move, and you need at least 2 different moves.

Your recordings live only in this browser tab. Refreshing the page wipes them, so don't refresh halfway through.

STEP 3 Train the model

This trains a small neural network with TensorFlow.js. Nothing gets sent anywhere — the training happens on this computer.

Red line = loss (how wrong it still is — should fall). Green line = accuracy (should rise).

STEP 4 Recognise live

Hit start, then do one of the moves you trained and see whether the AI can name it.
