📈 What does motion look like?

You just felt it with your hands — tilt the board, the ball rolls. Now let's open the computer's eyes and see what that tilt actually looks like inside: it's just a few numbers, jumping around.

0.00
X axis
0.00
Y axis
0.00
Z axis
X (left / right) Y (front / back) Z (up / down)
Lying flat and still, Z reads about 1 while X and Y read about 0 — that's Earth's gravity pulling down on it the whole time. Try standing the board on its edge, or flipping it over, and watch which line moves.
